About

Whether it runs is the easy part.

I care how things look, feel and behave, which is a big part of why I like building from scratch: every decision, from the data model to the hover state, is mine to make.

Right now that means VibePost, a social platform with no algorithm built to keep you scrolling. It lives under Tatom, the company I own, alongside BaldBot and the archived BlockCoin.

Outside of that I keep poking at AI agents, automation and self-hosting, mostly in Python. Code, learn, repeat.
